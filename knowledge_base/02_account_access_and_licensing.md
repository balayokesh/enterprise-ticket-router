# Account Access, Credentials & Software Licensing SOP

## Section 1: Resolving 'Invalid Credentials' Login Errors
When a customer reports an 'Invalid Credentials' error despite entering the correct password, instruct them to clear browser cache/cookies, verify Caps Lock is off, and check whether their account uses Single Sign-On (SSO) versus a local password.

## Section 2: Password Reset & Account Lockout Recovery
After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, accounts lock automatically for 15 minutes as a security precaution. Customers can trigger a self-service password reset link from the login page; reset tokens expire after 30 minutes. If the customer has contacted support multiple times without receiving reset emails, escalate to Tier-2 Identity Support to verify domain email blocks.

## Section 3: Software License Activation (Microsoft Office & AutoCAD)
For desktop software products such as Microsoft Office and Autodesk AutoCAD, ensure the customer is signed in with the exact email address tied to their active subscription seat. Deactivate unused device activations in the Account Portal if the seat limit has been reached.
