# Hardware Power, Charging & Battery Troubleshooting Runbook

## Section 1: Device Not Powering On (Dell XPS & Laptops)
When a laptop or portable device fails to power on despite using the original charger, instruct the user to perform a hard power drain: disconnect the AC adapter, hold the power button down for 20 seconds, reconnect the original charger directly to a wall outlet (bypassing surge protectors or docks), and attempt to boot.

## Section 2: Original Charger Not Charging Properly (Switch & Laptops)
If a device such as a Dell XPS or Nintendo Switch is not charging with the OEM charger, inspect the USB-C/power port for lint or bent pins, test an alternate wall outlet, and leave the device plugged in for at least 30 minutes to recover from a deeply discharged battery state. If charging still fails, flag for hardware repair/RMA.

## Section 3: Sudden Battery Drain & Post-Reset Persistence
For rapid battery degradation on smart devices (Philips Hue, Fitbit Versa, laptops), check background sync settings and recent firmware updates. If the user has already performed a factory reset and battery drain persists, treat as a hardware battery fault and escalate for warranty evaluation.
