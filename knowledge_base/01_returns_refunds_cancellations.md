# Return, Refund & Subscription Cancellation Policy

## Section 1: Standard 30-Day Hardware Return Window
Customers may return physical electronics (including Dell XPS laptops, LG Smart TVs, GoPro cameras, Nintendo Switch consoles, and Dyson appliances) within 30 calendar days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must include original chargers and accessories.

## Section 2: Defective Hardware & Hardware Noise Escalation
If a device exhibits abnormal mechanical noises (such as Dyson motor issues), fails to power on, or remains defective after a factory reset within 14 days of delivery, the customer qualifies for an expedited replacement unit with a prepaid return shipping label. Support agents must verify the device serial number before issuing an RMA.

## Section 3: Software & Subscription Cancellations
Recurring software subscriptions (including Microsoft Office and Autodesk AutoCAD) can be canceled at any time from the Billing Portal. Cancellations stop future renewals immediately, while access remains active through the end of the current paid billing cycle.

## Section 4: Refund Processing Timelines
Approved hardware refunds are initiated within 2 business days of warehouse scan and reflect on the customer's statement within 5 to 7 business days. Approved software or duplicate-charge refunds process within 3 to 5 business days.
