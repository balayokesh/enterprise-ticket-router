# Connectivity, Peripherals & Firmware Troubleshooting Guide

## Section 1: Wi-Fi & Network Setup Failures (Nest Thermostat & Smart Devices)
If a Nest Thermostat or smart home device fails to detect or connect to available Wi-Fi networks during setup, verify that the router broadcasts a 2.4 GHz band (many IoT devices do not support 5 GHz-only SSIDs), disable MAC address filtering temporarily, and restart both the router and the device.

## Section 2: Intermittent Peripheral & Display Compatibility (LG Smart TV & Controllers)
For intermittent connection or peripheral compatibility issues on LG Smart TVs or game controllers (Xbox / Nintendo Switch Pro Controller), test a certified high-speed HDMI/USB cable, switch ports, power-cycle the display by unplugging it for 60 seconds, and re-pair Bluetooth accessories.

## Section 3: Issues Occurring After Firmware Updates
When a customer reports bugs or malfunctions immediately following a firmware update (e.g., Fitbit Versa Smartwatch, Sony PlayStation, GoPro Hero), check for a hotfix patch in the companion app, perform a soft reboot, and collect the current firmware build number before escalating to Engineering.

## Section 4: Data Loss & Cloud Sync Recovery
If a user reports data loss after an app crash or update, check local autosave/backup directories and verify cloud sync status in account settings before performing any reset or reinstallation steps.
